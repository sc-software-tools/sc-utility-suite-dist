Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to use PDF Knife for confidential legal or financial records?

Yes. PDF Knife runs entirely on your local machine and makes zero network calls during file operations. Your sensitive files are never uploaded, stored, or inspected by any third party.

Is there a file size limit?

No. Because PDF Knife processes files locally, there are no artificial file size caps, page limits, or hourly conversion throttles.

Can I reorder pages before merging?

Yes. Drag and drop PDF files in your list to arrange them into the exact sequence you want before generating the combined document.
