What it does

SC FileFlow at a glance

Works on Windows 10 and 11 and on Linux.

Rules that fit your files

Match by file type, name pattern, regular expression, size or age. Rename as you go with {date}, {year}, {month}, {name}, {ext} and {counter}. Order your rules, switch them on and off, and save or load rule sets. Ready-made presets: Downloads organiser, photo year/month sorter, invoice & receipt collector.

See it before it happens

A dry-run preview shows every move and rename before anything is touched. Optional duplicate detection skips files you already have.

Undo and recovery

Every batch is written to a journal as it runs. Undo the last batch in one click. If your PC crashes or loses power mid-batch, FileFlow finds the interrupted batch and lets you finish or roll it back.

Hands-free

Watch a folder and organise new files as they arrive, after preview or automatically.
