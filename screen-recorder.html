No. Screen Lens never stamps watermarks on your recordings, even in the free version.

Can it create animated GIFs directly?

Yes. You can capture any screen area and save it directly as an animated GIF, perfect for GitHub pull requests, Slack, Teams, or bug tickets.

Does it record system audio and microphone?

Yes. You can select your audio input device to record clear voiceovers or system sound alongside your screen.
