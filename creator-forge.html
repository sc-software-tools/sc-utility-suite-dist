What it does

SC Creator Forge at a glance

Works on Windows 10 and 11 and on Linux. On Linux, MP4 (H.264) video uses your system's H.264 encoder; without one, videos are exported as WebM.

Pick a preset, drop your files, export

YouTube Shorts / 9:16 vertical: vertical video, Shorts cover and a 16:9 thumbnail. YouTube 1080p + artwork pack, or a 4K (2160p) kit. Social & store artwork pack: 1280×720 thumbnail, 1920×1080 hero, 1080×1080 square and 1080×1920 story. Creator full social suite: adds Instagram 4:5 (1080×1350) and a 21:9 banner (2560×1080). Podcast / audio master: normalised WAV plus 320 kbps MP3 and square cover art. Audio + thumbnail only.
