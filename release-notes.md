## SC Utility Suite v1.0.0 - Production Release

SC Utility Suite is a collection of 7 high-performance, privacy-focused desktop utilities for Windows 10 and Windows 11.

### Included Applications
1. **Archive Unpacker**: Fast extraction and inspection for 7z, ZIP, TAR, GZ, BZ2 and RAR archives.
2. **Dup Scrub**: Multi-phase scan engine to find and safely clean duplicate files via the Recycle Bin.
3. **HEIC Studio**: Batch convert Apple HEIC/HEIF photos, WebP and AVIF to JPG, PNG and PDF.
4. **Media Trim**: Millisecond-accurate video trimming and lossless audio extraction with bundled FFmpeg.
5. **PDF Knife**: Split by page ranges, merge multiple documents, or burst into individual pages.
6. **Screen Lens**: Screen and GIF recorder with floating HUD and multi-monitor region capture.
7. **USB Flasher**: Bootable UEFI ISO writer and raw sector disk imaging tool.
