The Seven Applications

Each tool operates as an independent desktop application with dedicated Free and PRO tiers.

Archive Unpacker

High-speed multi-format decompression

  • Extracts ZIP, 7Z, TAR, GZ, BZ2, and RAR archives.
  • Browse archive folders and choose individual entries to extract.
  • Create ZIP archives from a file or a folder and its contents.
Free: Single-archive extraction and basic ZIP creation PRO: Batch extraction, TAR.GZ and Maximum compression

Dup Scrub

Multi-phase duplicate cleaner with safety locks

  • Matches file sizes, samples and full XxHash64 hashes; compares bytes before recycling.
  • Moves selected duplicate files to the Windows Recycle Bin.
  • Keep the oldest or newest copy with automatic duplicate selection.
Free: Scan folders and clean one duplicate group at a time PRO: Select and clean duplicates across groups

HEIC Studio

Offline Apple HEIC & AVIF batch converter

  • 100% offline batch conversion of HEIC, HEIF, WebP, and AVIF photos.
  • Automatic image orientation, adjustable quality and optional resizing.
  • Convert to JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF with parallel processing.
Free: Convert up to 5 images per batch PRO: Unlimited images per batch

Media Trim

Video trimmer, converter and audio extractor

  • Bundled FFmpeg supports a lossless video cut preset; cut points depend on source keyframes.
  • Extract audio to MP3 at 320 kbps or uncompressed WAV.
  • Supports MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI, and high-bitrate action camera footage.
Free: Single-file editing with all five presets PRO: Removes the upgrade strip

PDF Knife

Local document splitter, merger & burster

  • Split by custom page ranges (e.g. 1-4, 8, 12-15) or burst every page.
  • Merge multiple PDFs in custom sequence with zero cloud uploads.
  • Clean stream processing with zero watermarks or formatting corruption.
Free: 2-file merge & 1 split range PRO: Unlimited document merge & automated burst

Screen Lens

Desktop screen recorder & optimized GIF generator

  • Floating HUD recording bar with full-screen and region selection.
  • Records H.264 MP4 video or animated GIFs, with pause and resume.
  • Optional microphone audio for MP4; recording controls stay outside the saved capture.
Free: Up to 3 minutes at 15 or 30 FPS; no watermark PRO: 60 FPS and unlimited recording duration

Raw sector image writer & bootable media creator

Architecture & Privacy Guarantees

Your files are processed locally. Licensing uses the relevant purchase provider.

Local File Processing

Your photos, videos, disk images, and documents never leave your PC. File processing stays on your PC. PRO license checks and activation contact Microsoft Store or Gumroad.

No Background Analytics

No background analytics or third-party advertisements are active. Free editions show a local PRO upgrade strip. Standalone activation saves a license cache on your Windows account.

Recycle Bin Safety

Destructive file operations (such as Dup Scrub duplicate cleaning) route files through the native Windows Recycle Bin with instant undo recovery.
