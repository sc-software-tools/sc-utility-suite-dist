Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Dup Scrub know two files are exact duplicates?

Dup Scrub uses a multi-stage verification process: first matching exact file sizes in bytes, then sampling headers, and finally comparing full SHA-256 cryptographic hashes. Two files must be 100% byte-for-byte identical to be flagged as duplicates.

Is it safe? What if I delete the wrong file?

Dup Scrub moves files directly to the standard Windows Recycle Bin rather than deleting them permanently. If you ever change your mind, you can open your Recycle Bin and restore them instantly.

Can it find duplicate photos with different filenames?

Yes. Because Dup Scrub inspects actual file content rather than just filenames, identical photos downloaded under different names will be accurately matched.
