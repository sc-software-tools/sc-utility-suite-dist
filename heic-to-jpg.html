Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't Windows open my iPhone photos by default?

Apple devices save photos in High Efficiency Image Container (.HEIC) format to save storage. Windows requires specialized extensions or an offline converter like HEIC Studio to convert them into universal JPG or PNG files.

Are my personal photos uploaded anywhere?

Never. HEIC Studio is 100% offline. All conversions run locally on your PC, ensuring complete privacy for family photos, sensitive documents, and personal albums.

What formats can HEIC Studio convert to?

You can convert HEIC, HEIF, WebP, and AVIF files into standard JPG or PNG images.
