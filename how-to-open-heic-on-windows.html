How to Open and Convert HEIC Photos on Windows 10 and 11

Photos copied from an iPhone or iPad usually end in .HEIC. Windows often shows them as blank icons or says it needs an extension. Here are three ways to fix it, from the quickest to the most flexible.

Option 1: Install Microsoft's HEIF and HEVC extensions

Windows can open HEIC photos in the Photos app once two extensions from the Microsoft Store are installed: HEIF Image Extensions (free) and HEVC Video Extensions (a small one-off charge). This lets you view photos, but other programs and websites still may not accept HEIC files.

Option 2: Make your iPhone save JPG in future

On the iPhone open Settings > Camera > Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos are saved as JPG. This doesn't change photos you've already taken, and the files are larger.

Option 3: Convert HEIC to JPG on your PC (free, offline)

If you need ordinary JPG or PNG files for email, printing or uploading, convert them. Online converters work, but they upload your personal photos to someone else's server. HEIC Studio converts on your own PC and includes its own decoder, so it works without the paid extensions.

Step by step with HEIC Studio

  1. Install HEIC Studio. Get it free from the Microsoft Store or as an installer, portable ZIP or Linux package from the HEIC Studio page.
  2. Add your photos. Drag HEIC photos, or a whole folder, into the window, or click Add Files.
  3. Choose a format. Pick JPG (best for sharing), PNG, WebP or PDF, and set the quality.
  4. Remove location if sharing. Tick Remove location (GPS) to strip where the photo was taken.
  5. Convert. Click Convert Files. The date taken, camera and colour profile are kept and photos are turned upright.

Questions

Is converting HEIC to JPG free?

Yes. HEIC Studio converts up to 5 photos per batch for free, with every format. A one-time PRO upgrade removes the batch limit.

Will I lose quality?

JPG is compressed, so set the quality slider to 90 or higher for near-identical results, or choose PNG for no further loss.

Do my photos get uploaded?

No. HEIC Studio runs entirely on your computer.
