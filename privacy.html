Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 · SC Software

Privacy Summary: SC Software applications operate on a strict 100% offline, local-first model. Your photos, videos, files, and screen recordings never leave your device. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data or usage analytics to remote servers.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the software applications and tools provided by SC Software (developed by Martin Wright), accessible on Android and Windows platforms, including:

HEIC Studio (com.scsoftware.heicstudio) Dup Scrub (com.scsoftware.dupscrub) Media Trim (com.scsoftware.mediatrim) Screen Lens (com.scsoftware.screenlens) PDF Knife (com.scsoftware.pdfknife) Archive Unpacker (com.scsoftware.archiveunpacker) USB Flasher (com.scsoftware.usbflasher)

2. Local-Only Processing (No Cloud Conversion)

All core functionalities performed by our applications take place exclusively within your device's local memory and processor:

  • HEIC Studio: Image decoding, format conversion, and EXIF processing happen entirely on-device.
  • Dup Scrub: File indexing, duplicate comparison, and visual preview generation remain strictly local.
  • Media Trim: Video decoding, trimming, and audio extraction are executed on-device using local decoders.
  • Screen Lens: Screen captures and audio narration are recorded and encoded directly to your local storage.
  • PDF Knife: PDF page extraction, splitting, merging, and image conversions are performed locally.
  • Archive Unpacker: Archive extraction (ZIP, RAR, 7z, TAR) occurs strictly to your chosen local directory.
  • USB Flasher: Writing disk images (ISO, IMG) to USB drives occurs directly through local hardware interfaces.

3. Android System Permissions & Data Usage

To deliver local productivity functionality, our Android applications request standard platform permissions. These permissions are used solely in real time for user-initiated operations:

  • Storage Access (Storage Access Framework / Media): Requested to enable the system file picker so you can select input files and designate output destination folders. The applications do not scan or catalog unrelated files outside your chosen selections.
  • Screen Capture / Media Projection (Screen Lens): Used strictly when you explicitly tap "Record Screen". Android displays a mandatory system consent dialog before any capture begins. Screen video is streamed directly to a local file and is never broadcast over the network.
  • Audio Recording / Microphone (Screen Lens): Optional permission requested only if you choose to record microphone voiceover alongside screen video. Audio data is mixed locally into the output video.
  • USB Host / OTG Access (USB Flasher): Requested when a USB flash drive or memory card reader is connected via USB-OTG. This access is used strictly to read drive capacity and write your selected image file to the USB drive.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications: Used solely to maintain background execution while you perform longer batch tasks (such as batch photo conversion or disk image flashing) and to display a non-intrusive progress notification.

4. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

SC Software offers optional lifetime PRO upgrades. On Android devices, all purchase transactions are processed exclusively through Google Play In-App Billing:

  • SC Software does not collect, receive, or store your credit card details, debit card details, or billing address. All financial transactions are managed securely by Google.
  • Google Play provides our apps with an anonymized cryptographic purchase token used solely to unlock PRO features on your device and restore purchases across your devices.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Analytics

Our applications respect your attention and device resources:

  • Zero Advertising SDKs: We do not integrate third-party advertising networks, ad banners, or commercial tracking libraries.
  • Zero Behavioral Tracking: We do not monitor user behavior, search queries, or tool usage across sessions.
  • Clean Navigation: Cross-promotion of other SC Software utilities is limited to simple static menu links, without trackers or ad networks.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children under the age of 13. Because our apps do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect or solicit information from children.

7. Data Security and Retention

Because SC Software does not transmit, store, or host your files or personal information on remote servers, your files remain under your complete control on your local hardware at all times.
